Job Title: Data Analyst (Junior to Mid-Level)

Company: Delta Insights
Location: Giza, Egypt (On-site, 3 days/week hybrid option)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role:
Delta Insights is hiring a Data Analyst to turn raw business data into actionable insights.
You will work with sales, marketing, and operations teams to build dashboards, run analyses,
and support data-driven decision-making across the company.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean, and validate data from multiple sources (databases, spreadsheets, APIs).
- Write SQL queries to extract and aggregate data from relational databases.
- Build interactive dashboards and reports using Power BI or Tableau.
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) using Python (pandas, numpy) or R.
- Present findings to stakeholders in clear, non-technical language.
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for process improvement.
- Maintain data documentation and ensure data quality standards.
- Collaborate with the data engineering team on ETL pipeline requirements.
- Support A/B testing and basic statistical analysis for marketing campaigns.

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, Business, or related field.
- 0-2 years of experience in a data analysis or business intelligence role.
- Strong SQL skills (joins, aggregations, window functions, subqueries).
- Proficiency in Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, formulas).
- Working knowledge of Python or R for data manipulation and basic analysis.
- Experience with at least one BI/visualization tool (Power BI, Tableau, Looker, or
  Google Data Studio).
- Understanding of basic statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, correlation,
  hypothesis testing).

Nice to Have:
- Experience with cloud data warehouses (BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift).
- Familiarity with version control (Git) for analytics code.
- Exposure to machine learning concepts (regression, classification) is a plus but not required.
- Knowledge of dbt or other data transformation tools.
- Experience with survey design or A/B testing frameworks.

Soft Skills:
- Strong attention to detail and data accuracy.
- Ability to translate business questions into analytical tasks.
- Clear written and verbal communication for presenting insights to non-technical audiences.
- Curiosity and a habit of questioning data before trusting it.

Salary Range: Competitive, based on experience.
Benefits: Health insurance, learning stipend, flexible hours, hybrid work option.
